Everything below is the Polyfork licence. It covers every asset on this site, the free mesh files too for anyone who would rather not credit us.

Linking to the free models on our CDN is not that, see below, and the free mesh files are yours to share under CC BY 4.0 instead, with the credit. Your game can ship the models inside it; the files as standalone assets stay here.

Products where other people build with them

Building a competing generator

Do not use anything you get here to build or train a commercial asset generator: a model, service, tool or pipeline that produces 3D assets and that you sell, license or offer to other people. That covers using the files as training, fine-tuning, evaluation or benchmark data, and it covers using them as the raw material a generator learns its output from. It applies to the ES modules, the HD editions, the renders and the text that ships beside them, and to the free mesh files for anyone using them under this licence, used directly or after modification. CC BY 4.0 sets no such limit on the free mesh files, and we will not pretend it does.

This is a limit on selling a generator, not on learning. The modules are published as readable source because that is what makes them worth having: an asset here is a program you can call, tune and read. Studying how one is built, training something on your own machine, running experiments, publishing research, writing your own generator for your own projects: all fine, and the source is open to you precisely so you can. What is not allowed is turning this catalog into a product that competes with it.

What you keep if your subscription ends

Everything you downloaded while subscribed is yours permanently, under this same license, with no attribution and no expiry. That is not a courtesy we can withdraw: it is the license the files were delivered under, and it applies whether you cancel, let a card lapse, or we stop selling subscriptions entirely. The single exception is breaking the terms above, which is covered below.

What stops is the part that was always a live service: your per-account CDN for the HD files, the uncapped remix budget, and HD editions published after your subscription ends. Access to those continues for 30 days past the end of your paid period, for a cancellation and a failed payment alike, so a declined card never takes a live site down without warning. The low poly models stay free either way.

You can take what you use with you at any time: every low poly model and every kit downloads as files on a free account, and the HD editions download one by one while you are on Pro. Lifetime and Founders accounts can also take the whole catalog as a single download from their account. We would rather you stayed because it is convenient than because leaving is hard.
